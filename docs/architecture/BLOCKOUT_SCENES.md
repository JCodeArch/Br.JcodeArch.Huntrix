# Standalone playable blockout compositions — card #60

Nine scene/prefab pairs under `Assets/Scenes/*_Blockout.unity` and `Assets/Resources/Levels/*_Blockout.prefab` compose each authored area with its geometry builder, a player slot, checkpoint flow, three-character roster, camera and prototype controls. `BlockoutLevelController2D` creates the stage before binding/spawning Rumi, then installs trigger regions for the authored checkpoint anchors. Loading another area cancels the current slot before disposing its old geometry. Stage/load notifications are isolated, and disable/reentry cannot spawn a stale player after ownership is lost.

`BlockoutPlayerInput2D` supplies keyboard/gamepad logical movement, jump, dash and melee attack; keyboard also provides parry and character switching. A/D or arrows move, Space jumps, Shift dashes, J attacks, K parries and Q switches. On-screen development buttons provide basic left/right/jump, and the area button cycles the nine fixtures. Ground sensing uses a bounded overlap query, rejects saturation and ignores the actor's own root. The camera follows only the current active actor. A geometric silhouette makes the runtime player visible; this is an original stand-in, not final character art.

Only standalone blockout prefabs attach this input adapter. `StageBlockoutBuilder2D` never auto-attaches input, and the vertical slice uses its separate input owner; both must not drive the same slot. Physical controls, mobile UX, final Zoey/Mira bindings, wall sensors and age/accessibility adaptations belong to their respective integrations.

All nine areas have distinct authored layouts. Most preserve a continuous lower route while optional terraces test verticality. Naksan intentionally uses a stepped climb to its raised arena and exit; its checkpoint destinations match raised ledges. Geometry is provisional and not nine finished campaign levels. Final enemy composition, visual polish, camera constraints, pacing and narrative remain separate work.

Structural inspection covers scene/prefab GUIDs, local/external references, serialized fields, dynamic player composition and one input owner. No Unity import, compilation, traversal, EditMode/PlayMode suite or playtest has run. Pending checks include platform reachability and collisions, checkpoint activation/respawn, callback disable/reentry, keyboard/gamepad/button control, jump cuts, camera framing, switches and all nine area loads.

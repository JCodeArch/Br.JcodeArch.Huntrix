# Flying enemy prototype — card #34

Source: [34 — Criar inimigos voadores](https://trello.com/c/tYNazBxA), “Voadores com mergulho, perseguição, projéteis e variação de altitude.” Uses the card #33 enemy foundation without changing its shared API.

Instantiate `Resources/Enemies/FlyingDemon_Prototype` as a scene root and explicitly supply a living opposing target through `EnemyAgent2D.TrySetTarget`. The root is a dynamic Demon receiver with gravity 0, capsule/hurtbox, protection opt-in, EnemyAgent, one FlyingEnemyController and geometric telegraph visual. Do not add GroundEnemyController or another Tick driver.

`FlyingEnemyDefinition` references a valid EnemyDefinition and projectile prefab. Authored prototype values: base chase speed 3, attack range 6, detection 12; hover offset 3 ± 0.75 world units; sine phase rate 0.5 radians/s; dive speed 8, deadline 0.5s; projectile speed 7, lifetime 2s, range 14. Attack damage 8 and centered 2×2 contact shape remain provisional. Range 6 reaches the authored hover band (maximum offset 3.75).

The controller pursues the explicit target at a variable altitude, returns to the hover band after a dive, and telegraphs before alternating dive/contact attack and projectile attack. During windup it stops, displays the shared yellow cue, and waits for EnemyAgent Telegraph→Attacking. Dive direction is captured when committed and has a fixed deadline; walls abort motion and a contact attack occurs through the shared bounded melee query at landing/deadline. The dive close-distance threshold uses half the smaller hitbox dimension, not full width. A moving target can dodge. Physics is responsible for body collision; there is no navmesh/pathfinding or obstacle routing.

`EnemyProjectile2D.TryLaunch(attack, owner, origin, aim, speed, lifetime, range, mask)` rejects invalid/dead/disabled owners, invalid direction and repeat launch. Each physics step sweeps a 32-contact ray across the movement distance, including triggers; query saturation destroys the projectile conservatively. The first living hurtbox consumes it through `Hurtbox2D.ResolveHit` (including faction rejection, parry, shield and dash); ordinary solid geometry also consumes it. Dead or disabled solid bodies can block but receive no damage. Lifetime and range both cap travel; a fractional final step respects the remaining lifetime. No reflection or piercing is introduced. Impact payload uses snapshots so owner-death callbacks cannot erase its source/attack.

At most four projectiles are owned per flyer. Death/disable/lost target cancels actions and owned projectiles; controller rechecks target/source after callback-producing state transitions and projectile instantiation. A retarget that cancels the agent attack cancels the dive on the next driver tick. Projectiles independently reject disabled/dead owners every step. Runtime Instantiate/Destroy and one placeholder Sprite per projectile remain until the pooling/performance cards (#95–96); no device budget is claimed.

Unity compilation/import and executable validation are deferred to the final phase by user instruction.

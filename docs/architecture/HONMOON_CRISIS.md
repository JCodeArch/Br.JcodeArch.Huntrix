# Honmoon crisis and union prototype — card #48

`HonmoonCrisisController2D` subscribes to an explicit Honmoon resource and applies a real intensity override to an explicit `HordeController2D`. The authored prototype halves spawn interval and adds four concurrent enemy slots, capped by the horde's hard 64-actor ceiling. Total spawn count remains bounded by the original definition; no catch-up burst is introduced. Only one enabled source may own the intensity override. Disabled/destroyed sources cannot continue intensifying, and exiting crisis removes only that source's override.

This is a runtime budget/cadence effect, not an empty event hook. Lowering the cap on crisis exit stops additional spawns until existing live count falls below the base cap; it does not destroy live actors to enforce the lower cap. CPU, allocations and device frame budgets remain unmeasured.

`TryUnite()` consumes an explicitly assigned `ITeamUnionAction2D`. One accepted action is allowed per crisis generation. Reservation occurs before invoking external action callbacks; a failed action restores the reservation only if the same generation remains current. Changed resource generation or disabled ownership prevents stale recovery effects. An accepted union restores 40 provisional Honmoon units and publishes `TeamUnited`; the effect is independent of final Special cost rules.

The concrete `CrisisTeamUnion2D` action works before card #49: it temporarily protects the active actors of one to three assigned player slots through `DamageProtection2D`'s explicit source contract. It validates unique enabled slots and living HuntrX receivers, grants two seconds of provisional protection and removes only its own registrations on expiry/disable. Three colored geometric indicators represent the trio in solo as well as multi-slot composition; it does not spawn inactive playable characters or establish a network/cooperative mode. It does not protect fans or claim the final Special sequence. Card #49's Special can implement the same interface and replace this assigned prototype action.

`Resources/Honmoon/HonmoonCrisisEncounter_Prototype.prefab` composes the existing player slot/checkpoint/start/floor/horde encounter with resource, crisis controller and concrete union. Development-only GUI buttons drain/recover 20 units and invoke union, making the resource transition and horde change inspectable without final platform input. These controls are a lab aid, not the final product interface.

All balance values are authored and validated. No Unity execution or device measurement was performed. Deferred checks include entry/exit hysteresis, actual effective spawn cadence/cap, total budget, competing sources, disabled-source cleanup, successful/rejected/reentrant union, three-slot validation, protection expiry and overlap with Mira, character switching/respawn and adapter replacement by the Special.

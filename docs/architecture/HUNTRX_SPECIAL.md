# HUNTR/X Special — card #49

Source: [49 — Implementar Special](https://trello.com/c/PVbkIfDF), “Medidor, carga, ativação e ataque conjunto.” `Resources/Special/HuntrXSpecial_Prototype` provides a wired SpecialDefinition with explicit runtime owner/target enrollment; it performs actual combat rather than only publishing an animation hook.

Configure with `TryConfigure(definition, livingHuntrXOwner, enemies, out error)` or use an authored ownerSlot and `TrySetTargets`. The target roster is copied, limited to 64 distinct receivers and may be updated only outside activation. Destroyed references are skipped during activation; a malformed serialized oversized roster fails closed. No Find/global player singleton is used.

Meter: MaximumCharge 100; +10 per enrolled Demon defeat, or explicit finite positive `TryAddCharge`. This is a provisional **team encounter defeat charge** rule, not last-hit attribution: all enrolled Demon deaths charge the meter regardless of killer. Fan deaths never charge it. Defeats during activation do not recharge this same Special. Charge values clamp to the authored maximum. ChargeChanged supplies committed values; activation and configuration are blocked during callback dispatch to prevent reentrant double effects.

Activation requires a living enabled HuntrX owner, ready meter, no active dash/melee/parry/Mira field/Zoey cooldown, and at least one eligible target. It reserves source/charge/target selection before callbacks. Eligible targets are living active Demon receivers within range 10, explicitly excluding FanActor in their parent or child hierarchy **even if mis-authored Demon faction**. Stable nearest-first selection chooses ceil(eligible×0.4), at least one, capped by the eligible roster. The historical approximate 40% concept is provisional authoring; damage protection, health and dodging can reduce actual defeat count.

A timed sequence emits StageChanged Rumi→Mira→Zoey→Combined. Each stage performs one shared Hurtbox.ResolveHit per selected eligible receiver: authored damage 3/3/3/120, duration 0.3s each. The Special is an authored area strike, not a ground hitbox or line-of-sight melee attack; it intentionally does not raycast walls. Shared contact semantics retain faction rejection, parry, protection and dash invulnerability. Fan exclusion, active source and range are rechecked before every stage/contact. A receiver resolves only once in each stage; there is no repeated collider damage.

Solo projects the same three performer identities; no inactive playable character is spawned, healed, forcibly moved, or treated as a new network participant. One core can represent the configured local team's joint resource; networking, final coop cost/synchronization and balance remain future decisions. ITeamUnionAction2D.TryExecuteTeamUnion delegates to TryActivate, allowing the Honmoon crisis action adapter to use the same combat Special without forward references from crisis card #48.

Death, disable, or changing the ownerSlot active actor cancels the sequence. Charge is already consumed and is not refunded. The owner may move during the sequence; range is rechecked. Completed(cancelled) is a lifecycle/presentation boundary. There is no implicit invulnerability, immobilization, physical input or cinematic pause. Reconfiguration, target mutation, charging and activation are blocked while resolving/dispatching. All event subscriber exceptions are isolated.

The core prefab deliberately has no scene references; scene composition must provide the live owner and enemy roster. Executable validation is deferred to the final phase: readiness/kill charge; 0/1/64/oversized targets; fractional selection and dedup; every stage; mixed-faction Fan exclusion; dead/disabled/destroyed references; shield/parry/dash; damage-callback cancellation; target motion; owner switch; no double consumption; 1–3-player composition; crisis integration; final boss and device performance. No Unity/test/device pass is claimed.

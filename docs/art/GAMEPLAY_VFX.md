# Gameplay VFX prototype

Card73 implements six original procedural effects: gold impact ring, cyan charge line, violet Honmoon ring, rose expanded Special ring, mint fan→drainer soul line, and orange enemy telegraph ring. GameplayVfxAdapter2D consumes accepted DamageDealt/DamageReceived, Honmoon band transitions, Special charge/stage events, FanDrainAccepted and EnemyState.Telegraph. No visual overlap creates damage or changes resources. Special stage also requests the animator special pose. Sources are explicit serialized references or TryConfigure arguments; dynamic actors require explicit rebinding by the scene owner.

GameplayVfxPool2D bounds active lines to authored32 (hard64), with fixed12 ring vertices, finite world coordinates, duration0.55s and monotonic fade. Full pools drop additional cosmetic emissions. No strobe or fullscreen flashes. Material and owned children clean up on destroy; disable expires all active effects. RumiVfx prefab is a combat-ready animated actor with accepted-hit adapter and optional development buttons for six shape previews. Later scene bridge binds shared Honmoon/Special and current player. Shader Sprites/Default availability/render pipeline, event timing, pooling behavior and camera readability require final Unity validation. These are prototype VFX, not polished production effects.

## Final composition accessibility policy

The explicit card93 binder supplies active actor age-profile VisualIntensity and reduced-flash preference through GameplayVfxAdapter2D.SetPresentationPolicy(float intensity, bool reduceFlashes, bool validProfile=true). Invalid/nonfinite profiles fail closed. Changing intensity/preference/profile validity clears all already-active pooled effects immediately. The binder also calls ClearActiveEffects on actor/profile transitions even when their numerical policy matches. Reduced flashes suppress decorative impact, energy, Honmoon, Special and soul-drain visuals; gameplay and special poses still run. Essential enemy telegraphs remain a steady ring at gentle .25 RGB brightness without expansion/fade modulation during their bounded lifetime. Otherwise authored age intensity scales effect brightness; telegraph brightness has a .25 readability floor. This is explicit composition, with no scene lookup or card73 dependency on age-selector types. Render contrast and accessibility/device behavior require final Unity validation.

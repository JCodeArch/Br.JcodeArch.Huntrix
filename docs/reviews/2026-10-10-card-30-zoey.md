## Implemented scope

- `ZoeyRangedDefinition` validates the shared attack, finite positive range/cooldown, and finite muzzle offset.
- `ZoeyRangedAttack2D.TryFire` accepts an explicit world-space direction for horizontal, diagonal, and aerial aiming. It resolves the nearest eligible ray contact through the existing hurtbox contract.
- Solid geometry blocks shots; unrelated triggers and self colliders are ignored. A full 64-contact buffer rejects the shot conservatively.
- Shot admission rejects invalid/dead/disabled actors, conflicting dash/melee/parry, cooldown, and re-entry. Presentation events expose emitted shots, accepted damage, and parry without introducing art/audio dependencies.
- Authored character profile, attack/ranged definitions, prefab, and `.meta` files provide a separate Zoey prototype. Shared movement/melee/parry assets are provisional fixtures.

# Air Dash Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (- [ ]) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add the approved single-use directional air dash to the HUNTR/X 2D dash controller.

**Architecture:** DashController2D owns air-dash charge and lifecycle. At start, it applies normalized 2D velocity once and acquires the existing owner-scoped horizontal override for the duration. Rigidbody2D gravity continues integrating Y. Air dash temporarily suppresses JumpController2D so jump start and jump cut cannot replace the dash impulse.

**Tech Stack:** Unity 6, C#, Unity Test Framework PlayMode.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-air-dash-design.md

## Global Constraints
- Use Move as logical Vector2 input; never add device bindings.
- Normalize direction; apply configured air speed at dash start without inventing values or cooldown.
- Keep gravity active and do not rewrite Y in the dash loop.
- Preserve the existing horizontal-only owner override and ground-dash contract.
- One air dash per airborne interval, replenished by SetGrounded(true).
- Suppress buffered/coyote jump and jump cut during air dash; restore jump behavior at termination.

## Review Focus
- Diagonal/non-finite/zero direction: verify normalized magnitude and rejection without consuming charge.
- Competing override owner and cleanup: verify failed acquisition is side-effect free.
- FixedUpdate expiry and gravity: verify exact lifetime, constant X ownership, and vertical gravity integration.
- Landing during dash and disable: verify idempotent recharge and exactly one end transition.
- Coyote/buffered jump and jump release: verify air-dash priority independent of FixedUpdate order.

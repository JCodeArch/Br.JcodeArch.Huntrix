# Combo, Hit Stop, and Impact Feedback Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Deliver card #25's configurable ground/air chains, accepted-hit hit stop, and typed impact feedback event.

**Architecture:** `CombatComboDefinition` stores explicit grounded/aerial `ComboStep` sequences. `AttackController2D` selects one context per chain and accepts one logical Attack press only inside a link window. `AttackHitbox2D` owns per-step deduplication and emits only an internal accepted-hit notification; the enabled `AttackController2D` receives it, requests hit stop, then publishes the public typed event. The service is the sole owner of global `Time.timeScale` during a stop.

**Tech Stack:** Unity 6 / C#, ScriptableObject data, Unity 2D Physics, Unity Test Framework EditMode and PlayMode.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-combo-design.md`

## Constraints

- Do not author sample combo assets, final timing/damage values, or final balance.
- Keep logical Attack as a press edge; no physical binding, hold-repeat, queue outside the link window, or multi-step advancement from one press.
- Ground/air context is caller-supplied and fixed for one chain; each new chain selects its context anew.
- Whiffs can chain inside the configured window; hit confirmation is not a prerequisite.
- Accepted targets are deduplicated once per step; preserve all card #24 combat rules.
- Global hit stop is a single-player prototype choice, not an approved local-co-op policy; revisit its global scope before local co-op. It uses one pre-scene coordinator with pause API, unscaled max deadline, explicit ownership loss, prior base-scale restoration, no per-hit coroutine, and no fixedDeltaTime changes.
- No VFX/SFX/animation assets, parry, counterattack, reward, character/scene wiring, or network behavior.

## Review Focus

- Chain window start/end are exact and derived from step start; no early/late acceptance, no chaining from final step, one edge equals one transition.
- Starting a chain with ground/air chooses the expected sequence and context does not switch mid-chain.
- Canceling a step closes its active hitbox and clears dedupe before next activation.
- Each step may hit a receiver once; later steps may hit it again; rejected contacts do not emit events or request hit stop.
- Hit stop preserves a non-default base scale; pause API overlays an active deadline; add explicit pending-deadline tests for pause/resume both before the unscaled deadline (resume must reapply the remaining freeze) and after it (resume must restore base scale without a new freeze); new requests while paused are ignored. A nonzero external write while the service expects zero, including during pause, relinquishes all time-scale ownership, preserves the external value, clears pause/freeze state, and discards pending deadlines. Disable/destroy while still owning pause or freeze restores captured base scale even when paused; no fixedDeltaTime mutation.
- No duplicate/stale runner, stuck timescale, buffer, event listener, hitbox, or delayed hit after disable/despawn; review the subsystem-registration reset hook for domain reload.
- Performance claims remain limited to code-path inspection; device targets and budgets are not approved.
